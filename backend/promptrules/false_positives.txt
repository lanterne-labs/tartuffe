It is NOT a contradiction when ANY of these false-positive patterns apply — these OVERRIDE a YES. Each is tagged with its rule token in brackets.

- [third_party_narrative] Applies ONLY when NEITHER statement gives the person's own words, position, attitude, or action AND neither is an authoritative finding (conviction, official conclusion) — e.g. two outlets describing an event the person never personally commented on. A statement of what the person said, declared, felt, promised, or did ("X said Y", "X s'est dit motivé", "X a déclaré Z") COUNTS as the person's own utterance — that is NOT this bucket, so do not drop it here.
- [different_subjects] Different subjects despite overlapping keywords (the person's own belief vs an external party's action; what Parliament did vs what the person told colleagues). A word used with DIFFERENT meanings across the two facts (a homonym, e.g. "primaire" = école primaire vs une primaire électorale) is NOT a shared subject. So is WHOSE commitment: "Trump keeps his commitments" and "Trump now sees that Russia does not keep its commitments" are about two different parties. EXCEPTION: denouncing or blaming a SPECIFIC NAMED person or party and then allying, merging lists, or partnering with that SAME target is a real reversal — answer YES.
- [different_events] Genuinely unrelated actions, bills, cases, or occasions. NOT this bucket: a stated commitment and a later action that walks it back (e.g. "promised to keep retirement at 62" vs "abandoned the repeal of the move to 64") — that is one broken commitment, answer YES.
- [numbers_measurements] Different counts, percentages, vote-shares, or poll figures of the same quantity at different times, sources, or electoral rounds.
- [forecast_vs_current] One is a forecast or projection about the future, the other a current or regional figure.
- [accusation_not_finding] An accusation, indictment, summons, or ongoing trial paired with a denial. Procedural steps are not guilt; only a conviction or official conclusion breaks a denial. A prosecutor's réquisition (requested sentence) is likewise only a request by a third party — it is neither a finding nor an act of self-exemption by the person.
- [legal_process_stages] Accusation, indictment, trial, and conviction are sequential stages of the SAME process; being accused then convicted is normal progression.
- [conviction_vs_acknowledgment] A conviction does not contradict admitting imperfections, error, or negligence; good faith is compatible with negligence. Only an explicit denial of the SAME wrongdoing a conviction then establishes is a contradiction.
- [complementary_facts] Different ASPECTS of the same ruling or event (prison term plus ineligibility; first-instance plus appeal sentence) are supplementary, not contradictory. Two compatible FACETS of one position expressed together are likewise not a reversal — e.g. a moral judgment ("he should have resigned") alongside a practical assessment ("but his resignation would serve no purpose") is one nuanced stance. So are two CAUSES of one failure: naming the PRIMARY culprit or ruling out ONE cause ("le premier responsable est le meurtrier, et non un manque de moyens") and later admitting OTHER failures (errors, negligence in the chain) — "premier" is not "seul", and errors are not a lack of means.
- [coherent_defense] Multiple denials about DIFFERENT aspects of one case form a single consistent defense. Only admitting a claim then denying that SAME claim is a contradiction.
- [procedural_scope] Different scopes or components of the SAME action (a 49-3 on the revenue part vs the expenditure part vs the overall budget). Procedure on different parts of one measure is not inconsistency. A targeted or PARTIAL measure for specific groups does not contradict declining the TOTAL or universal version of that same measure — "total"/"universelle"/"généralisée" marks a different scope (e.g. free transport for seniors and hospital staff vs ruling out fully free transport for everyone; doubling a tax-free fuel PREMIUM, or extending aid to the most exposed sectors, vs refusing a general fuel-tax cut or generalized fuel aid).
- [condition_later_met] One fact is conditional ("I would do X IF Y happens") and the other shows Y came true or X happening — that is consistent, not a contradiction. The SAME applies when the condition did NOT occur: if a DIFFERENT outcome happened (e.g. candidacy promised IF a SUSPENDED sentence, but the person received a FIRM sentence), the conditional was never triggered and the commitment is untouched. A conditional is broken ONLY if its exact antecedent occurred and the person then did the opposite of what they pledged. Whether the pledged action has happened YET is irrelevant and is never a reason to hedge: a court ruling or event that merely fulfils the IF-condition cannot contradict the pledge, so the answer is a firm NO / remove, not "ambiguous".
- [consistent_ignorance] Claims of not knowing about DIFFERENT aspects of one situation reinforce each other. Only demonstrated knowledge that another statement denies is a contradiction.
- [subjective_vs_objective] "I wasn't aware of X" (subjective) and "X didn't happen" (objective) are compatible; denying X does not imply prior knowledge of X.
- [paraphrase_same_position] Both statements take the SAME side or support the SAME claim, regardless of wording, language, or level of detail. Map each statement to the position it IMPLIES before comparing: opposing or criticizing a measure is the SAME position as supporting its opposite (e.g. "the statute of limitations protects perpetrators" means wanting non-prescription, which equals defending imprescriptibility). Do not read a double negative as a reversal. Resolve vocabulary before comparing: "imprescriptibilité"/"non-prescription" means NO statute of limitations, so defending imprescriptibilité and calling prescription protective of perpetrators are the SAME stance.
- [same_direction] Both facts point the SAME way. Includes follow-through (an action that FULFILLS, ADVANCES, or IMPLEMENTS a stated goal — if the reason says B "fulfills", "implements", or "is the concrete action of" A, answer NO) and criticism of non-enforcement (calling a rule "useless unless enforced" or "not applied" wants it applied, not opposed). Budget French: "un effort (supplémentaire) sur les dépenses" means spending CUTS, the same direction as a spending freeze, not more spending.
- [announcement_then_action] Announcing, promising, or planning something then DOING it is a fulfilled commitment. Contradictory only if the person did the OPPOSITE of what they announced.
- [vague_vs_specific] One statement vague or general, the other specific, with no clear sign they refer to the same thing. If context (names, dates, events) shows they ARE the same thing, it IS a contradiction. A QUALIFIED general position (opposing X "en permanence" / systematically / "as a rule") is compatible with endorsing ONE specific instance of X — the qualifier scopes the stance to repeated/systematic cases, so a single specific case does not break it. EXCEPTION: a normative RULE or SANCTION the person publicly demanded for a CLASS of people ("anyone convicted should be ineligible / must resign / should be barred") is NOT vague once the person themselves demonstrably enters that class — it binds them too, and their own act exempting themselves from that same sanction IS a contradiction (self-exempting double standard) — answer YES. This EXCEPTION requires BOTH sides to be the person's OWN words or acts: one side states the rule, the OTHER side must be them claiming or taking the exemption ("je suis éligible", "rien ne m'empêche d'être candidate", "j'annonce ma candidature"). If the other side is instead something DONE TO them by the justice system or by anyone else — a sentence, a court or appeal ruling, a prosecutor's request, an awaiting-verdict account — answer NO: undergoing the sanction is what their own rule prescribes, so it AGREES with the rule instead of breaking it.
- [geographic_scope] A regional, departmental, or local fact does not contradict national-level data.
- [learning_over_time] Earlier "didn't know" then later "knew" is consistent (learned over time). Earlier "claimed X" then later "denied X" IS a contradiction.
- [date_only_no_commitment] Only the date or timing of an event differs, with no personal commitment about that timing being broken. Default NO.
- [endorsement_vs_non_candidacy] Endorsing or backing ANOTHER named person for an office while stating you are not a candidate yourself: backer and candidate are compatible roles. Does NOT apply when the person themselves heads, leads, or conducts a list ("liste conduite par X", "tête de liste"), is elected, or wins seats — leading a list IS being a candidate, so "not a candidate" followed by leading a list is a real reversal (YES).
- [relative_vote_preference] AMENDMENT BALLOTS ONLY. A vote on an amendement/sous-amendement judges that modification, in context: voting it down implies neither support for the unamended text, nor opposition to the parent bill (rejecting a rival group's dilatory amendment to a bill one champions IS championing it), nor endorsement of the modification's opposite. Drop claims that invert an amendment vote's meaning. Never use this bucket for votes on the text itself (l'ensemble, article, motion), nor when the ballot was cast AGAINST the very measure the person explicitly demanded — that one breaks their own principle: keep.
- [symbolic_counter_gesture] A small, symbolic, or immaterial action in the other direction does not undermine a stated value; the action must MATERIALLY undermine the value on the same axis.
