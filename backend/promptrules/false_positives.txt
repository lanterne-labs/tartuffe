It is NOT a contradiction when ANY of these false-positive patterns apply — these OVERRIDE a YES. Each is tagged with its rule token in brackets.

- [third_party_narrative] Applies ONLY when NEITHER statement gives the person's own words, position, attitude, or action AND neither is an authoritative finding (conviction, official conclusion) — e.g. two outlets describing an event the person never personally commented on. A statement of what the person said, declared, felt, promised, or did ("X said Y", "X s'est dit motivé", "X a déclaré Z") COUNTS as the person's own utterance — that is NOT this bucket, so do not drop it here.
- [different_subjects] Different subjects despite overlapping keywords (the person's own belief vs an external party's action; what Parliament did vs what the person told colleagues). A word used with DIFFERENT meanings across the two facts (a homonym, e.g. "primaire" = école primaire vs une primaire électorale) is NOT a shared subject. EXCEPTION: denouncing or blaming a SPECIFIC NAMED person or party and then allying, merging lists, or partnering with that SAME target is a real reversal — answer YES.
- [different_events] Genuinely unrelated actions, bills, cases, or occasions. NOT this bucket: a stated commitment and a later action that walks it back (e.g. "promised to keep retirement at 62" vs "abandoned the repeal of the move to 64") — that is one broken commitment, answer YES.
- [numbers_measurements] Different counts, percentages, vote-shares, or poll figures of the same quantity at different times, sources, or electoral rounds.
- [forecast_vs_current] One is a forecast or projection about the future, the other a current or regional figure.
- [accusation_not_finding] An accusation, indictment, summons, or ongoing trial paired with a denial. Procedural steps are not guilt; only a conviction or official conclusion breaks a denial.
- [legal_process_stages] Accusation, indictment, trial, and conviction are sequential stages of the SAME process; being accused then convicted is normal progression.
- [conviction_vs_acknowledgment] A conviction does not contradict admitting imperfections, error, or negligence; good faith is compatible with negligence. Only an explicit denial of the SAME wrongdoing a conviction then establishes is a contradiction.
- [complementary_facts] Different ASPECTS of the same ruling or event (prison term plus ineligibility; first-instance plus appeal sentence) are supplementary, not contradictory.
- [coherent_defense] Multiple denials about DIFFERENT aspects of one case form a single consistent defense. Only admitting a claim then denying that SAME claim is a contradiction.
- [procedural_scope] Different scopes or components of the SAME action (a 49-3 on the revenue part vs the expenditure part vs the overall budget). Procedure on different parts of one measure is not inconsistency.
- [condition_later_met] One fact is conditional ("I would do X IF Y happens") and the other shows Y came true or X happening — that is consistent, not a contradiction.
- [consistent_ignorance] Claims of not knowing about DIFFERENT aspects of one situation reinforce each other. Only demonstrated knowledge that another statement denies is a contradiction.
- [subjective_vs_objective] "I wasn't aware of X" (subjective) and "X didn't happen" (objective) are compatible; denying X does not imply prior knowledge of X.
- [paraphrase_same_position] Both statements take the SAME side or support the SAME claim, regardless of wording, language, or level of detail. Map each statement to the position it IMPLIES before comparing: opposing or criticizing a measure is the SAME position as supporting its opposite (e.g. "the statute of limitations protects perpetrators" means wanting non-prescription, which equals defending imprescriptibility). Do not read a double negative as a reversal.
- [same_direction] Both facts point the SAME way. Includes follow-through (an action that FULFILLS, ADVANCES, or IMPLEMENTS a stated goal — if the reason says B "fulfills", "implements", or "is the concrete action of" A, answer NO) and criticism of non-enforcement (calling a rule "useless unless enforced" or "not applied" wants it applied, not opposed).
- [announcement_then_action] Announcing, promising, or planning something then DOING it is a fulfilled commitment. Contradictory only if the person did the OPPOSITE of what they announced.
- [vague_vs_specific] One statement vague or general, the other specific, with no clear sign they refer to the same thing. If context (names, dates, events) shows they ARE the same thing, it IS a contradiction.
- [geographic_scope] A regional, departmental, or local fact does not contradict national-level data.
- [learning_over_time] Earlier "didn't know" then later "knew" is consistent (learned over time). Earlier "claimed X" then later "denied X" IS a contradiction.
- [date_only_no_commitment] Only the date or timing of an event differs, with no personal commitment about that timing being broken. Default NO.
- [endorsement_vs_non_candidacy] Endorsing or backing ANOTHER named person for an office while stating you are not a candidate yourself: backer and candidate are compatible roles.
- [symbolic_counter_gesture] A small, symbolic, or immaterial action in the other direction does not undermine a stated value; the action must MATERIALLY undermine the value on the same axis.
